Privacy Policy

Effective Date: [May 26, 2026]

KvetchUP respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through our website, how we use it, and what choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily submit through our website, including your name, email address, message or booking inquiry, and any other information you choose to include in the contact form.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information, payment information, government identification numbers, or health information through this website.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit to respond to messages and booking inquiries, communicate with you about performances or availability, keep a record of inquiries for scheduling and administrative purposes, and improve our website and communication process.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. How Form Submissions Are Stored

When you submit the contact form, your submission may be sent to us by email and/or stored in a Google Sheet using Google Apps Script.

This means your information may be processed through Google services. You can review Google's Privacy Policy here: Google Privacy Policy .

4. Third-Party Services

Google Fonts

Our website may load fonts from Google Fonts. This may involve requests to Google servers in order to display the fonts used on this website.

YouTube Embeds

Our website may include embedded YouTube videos. We use YouTube's privacy-enhanced embed option where possible by using the youtube-nocookie.com domain.

Instagram Links

Our website may link to our Instagram profile. If you click that link, you will leave our website and Instagram's own privacy practices will apply.

5. Cookies and Tracking

We do not currently use advertising cookies, analytics cookies, or tracking pixels directly on this website.

However, third-party embedded content, such as YouTube videos, may process information when you interact with it. If this changes in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy.

6. How We Share Information

We may share your information only when reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry, coordinate performances or bookings, use trusted service providers such as Google to operate the contact form, or comply with legal obligations if required.

We do not sell, rent, or trade contact form submissions.

7. How Long We Keep Information

We keep contact form submissions for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, manage bookings, maintain records, and support band administration.

You may contact us at kvetchupband@gmail.com to request that we delete your contact form submission.

8. Your Choices

You may contact us to ask what information you submitted through the website, request correction of inaccurate information, request deletion of your contact form submission, or ask privacy-related questions.

Please email us at kvetchupband@gmail.com.

9. Children's Privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child under 13 has submitted personal information through our website, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

We use reasonable steps to protect contact form submissions. However, no website, email system, or online service can guarantee complete security.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date above.
